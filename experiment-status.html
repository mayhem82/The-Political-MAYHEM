EXPERIMENTAL CLAIM BOUNDARY

What can and cannot currently be said

Demonstrated now: forward-only source capture, immutable source-change lineage, explicit semantic review, projection freezes, probability precommitment, pre-freeze comparator validation, retained failures, jurisdiction separation, source provenance and an operational edge/calibration ledger.

Not demonstrated merely by architecture: superior political forecasting, persistent calibration advantage, or a positive edge over market/consensus baselines.

Required for empirical advantage: a growing sample of frozen probability tips, verified outcomes, valid pre-freeze comparison baselines and positive scoring performance retained over time.

Signal firewall: a page changing is not itself a political signal and cannot be used directly in a frozen projection.

Density requirement: the political engine must continue registering intermediate verifiable targets rather than waiting only for general elections.

No silent emptiness: unavailable runtime data is a data-availability failure, not evidence of zero activity.
