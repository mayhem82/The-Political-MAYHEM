Grade / audit

Field: politics never goes idle; the evidence environment is continuously changing.

Tip: the public-facing selected outcome for a defined political target.

Projection: the immutable analytical record underneath that tip.

Freeze: the last valid pre-outcome tip state for that target. Other political intelligence continues moving.

Grade: CORRECT, INCORRECT, PARTIAL or VOID against a verified outcome.
