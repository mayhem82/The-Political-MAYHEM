Tip: the public-facing selected political outcome.

Projection: the underlying immutable analytical record supporting that tip.

Freeze: the last valid pre-outcome tip state. It cannot be rewritten after the result.

Grade: CORRECT, INCORRECT, PARTIAL or VOID against a verified outcome.
