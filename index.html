Non-negotiable integrity rules

Only evidence knowable at the capture time can influence that projection.

Later evidence creates a later state. It does not rewrite an earlier one.

Frozen projections remain immutable, including failed projections.

Missed pre-outcome checkpoints are marked missed, not reconstructed.

Psychological or causal inference remains separate from observed fact.
