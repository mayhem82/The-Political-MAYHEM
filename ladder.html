What these views mean

Sports ladders need an objective ordering rule. Parliamentary teams are ranked only by a named source metric. The First Team is structurally different: elections and referendums are direct decision points; polling is sampled opinion, not a direct public result; silence between decision points is not approval. Parliamentary representation, government status, Senate balance and tipping probability remain separate dimensions and are never silently mixed into one score.
