Baseline first. A source's first captured representation establishes its forward baseline and creates neither a raw change event nor a political signal.

Source change is not a signal. A changed normalized representation is preserved as SOURCE_CHANGED, with before/after snapshot lineage and a reproducible delta hash.

Promotion is explicit. A political signal is a new append-only event linked to a separate review record and its parent source-change event. The raw observation is never rewritten.

Exclusion is retained. A reviewed source change judged non-signal remains in the audit record.

Projection firewall. Raw SOURCE_CHANGED events are prohibited from projection lineage. Only reviewed signals or independently observed facts can enter a frozen tip.

Legacy records remain immutable. Early automated SIG-WATCH possible-signal records are retained as originally captured; the stricter separation applies prospectively.
