Source health — all configured watchers

Reading watcher state

Persisted baseline and failure state will appear here.

Baseline first. A source's first captured representation establishes its forward baseline and creates neither a raw change event nor a signal.

Capture-mode migration is not evidence. Moving a watcher from whole-page capture to structured publication-feed capture creates a new baseline representation without manufacturing a source-change event.

Source change is not a signal. A changed representation is preserved as SOURCE_CHANGED with before/after snapshot lineage and a reproducible delta hash.

Structured additions can be reviewed deterministically. If an official publication index gains a new structured record, the review engine may promote only the fact that the record appeared. The publication's substantive claims are not thereby verified.

Unstructured changes stay pending. Parliament pages and other whole-page changes remain raw until human or assisted semantic review establishes what changed.

Removals are not reversals. A record disappearing from an index is retained as source movement but is not automatically treated as a political reversal or substantive fact.

Projection firewall. Raw SOURCE_CHANGED events cannot enter frozen tip lineage. Signal promotion itself also has NO_EFFECT until a registered target separately consumes reviewed evidence.
