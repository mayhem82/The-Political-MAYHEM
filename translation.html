FOUNDATIONAL TRANSLATION

The People → First Team

NON-PARLIAMENTARY

The First Team is the sport-legibility translation for the Australian public/electorate. It is not a party, does not occupy seats, and does not receive a fabricated citizen roster. “Ultimate sovereign / principal” is retained as constitutional-democratic interpretation rather than literal constitutional text.

The people / constitutional electorateFirst Team / foundational team — the public body outside the chamber that directly selects representatives and participates in constitutional referendums.
Parliamentary partiesCompeting teams claiming to represent, persuade or govern for the First Team. The claim itself is not evidence that the public agrees.
ElectionsFixtures / direct selection points where electors determine who enters or remains in the parliamentary field.
Sections 7 and 24Selection mechanism: senators and House members are constitutionally described as directly chosen by the people.
Section 128Special structural fixture: constitutional alteration requires the referendum process and elector approval required by the Constitution.
“Will of the people” / “the people have spoken”Post-fixture political language. Record the actual result separately from claims about its broader meaning.
“Ordinary Australians” / “public interest”Team or player positioning about the First Team until independently evidenced for the relevant question and population.
No public parliamentary rosterThe First Team remains outside the chamber. Citizens are not converted into synthetic player cards or aggregate parliamentary seats.
No continuous direct public decisionFirst Team form is intermittent and event-driven. Silence between decision points is not approval.
PollingSampled opinion evidence only. It may inform a narrow proposition but never substitutes for a direct electoral or referendum result.
FIRST TEAM CONTROLS

Translation boundaries

EVIDENCE LOCKED

No fake roster

No citizen player cards, fantasy statistics or synthetic parliamentary identity.

STRUCTURAL CONTROL

No party-ladder insertion

The First Team is excluded from the primary-vote party ladder and receives a separate sovereign/electoral form view.

METRIC CONTROL

No silence = consent

Between decision points, First Team position remains UNKNOWN unless narrower evidence supports a specific claim.

EVIDENCE CONTROL

No rhetoric = mandate

Claims to speak for Australians remain political positioning until independently evidenced.

CLAIM CONTROL

Polling stays polling

Sampled opinion cannot be translated into a direct constitutional or electoral decision.

PROXY CONTROL

Ground requires outcome

Ground gained or lost is recorded only from a verifiable public outcome or measurable citizen-driven institutional concession.

OUTCOME CONTROL

Sport for legibility. Teams, players, form, fixtures, tipping, First Team and results make the field readable.

Evidence architecture for truth control. Translation never alters timestamps, provenance, evidence state, constitutional wording or audit history.

What can actually speak for the First Team?

NO PROXY SUBSTITUTION

Political language about Australians is classified by evidence type before it can affect First Team state. A speaker claiming a mandate is not the same evidentiary object as an election result; a poll is not a referendum; participation is not proof of whole-population representativeness.
